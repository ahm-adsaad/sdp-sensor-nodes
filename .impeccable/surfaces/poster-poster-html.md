# Surface: EWSERC/SEWA competition poster

Scope: one A0 portrait poster (841 x 1189 mm), printed and submitted as PDF. Mode: Read.
Audience: workshop judges (SEWA, EWSERC, industry, academia) reading at 1-2 m, then up close.
Content: report only (paper/), no measured results, no captions, no subtitles.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An IEEE conference poster played straight and done precisely: numbered sections, booktabs tables, numbered equations, a ruled algorithm, numbered references. Refuses the infographic-poster default (icon tiles, stat blocks, gradient banners).

OWN-WORLD: White print ground, near-black ink, AUS maroon #880E12 owning the title band and section rules. Data colours: Rx blue #3A6EA5, sensing ochre #B8861B, Tx brick #B5373D, sleep warm gray. STIX Two Text throughout, STIX Two Math for equations, Source Sans 3 only inside diagrams.

STORY: Batteries limit scalable WSNs; the team couples solar EH, node virtualization and energy-aware task assignment; the reader sees the system, the node, the schedule, the MILP and heuristic, the design choices, and the evaluation targets.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width maroon title band: AUS seal left, title, authors, advisor, department. Below, three columns; column one opens with I. Motivation and the related-work gap table; the system diagram spans columns two and three.

FORM: brief-pinned IEEE poster; seed: none. No roll was made because the user pinned the form in the request ("ieee poster style").

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
